## Receive from phone

- Send photos and videos from your phone straight into your school folder, in their **original quality** (messaging
  apps recompress them). Everything goes over your own Wi-Fi: no cloud, no account.
- Click the new **phone** button in the top bar, switch receiving on, and scan the QR code with your phone's camera
  (or open the address in Safari and type the key).
- Choose the folder on the phone (or keep the default **Inbox**), pick your photos and videos, and watch each one
  arrive. NoteTOO shows them in your folder right away and never replaces a file that's already there.
- On iPhone, tap **Options** in the photo picker and choose **Format: Current** to keep HEIC photos as they are.
- The connection is protected by a key but not encrypted: use it on your home Wi-Fi, not on public networks, and
  switch it off when you're done. Windows may ask once whether NoteTOO can use the network: allow private networks.
