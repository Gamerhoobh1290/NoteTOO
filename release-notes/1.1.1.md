## More precise highlights

- **Highlight important** no longer marks bare fragments such as a single short list item: every highlight is a
  complete point of at least four words (formulas still count).
- A summary section ("À retenir", "Summary"…) gets at most one highlight, since it repeats the rest of the course;
  the highlights go to the sections where each idea is explained.

## NoteTOO Updater 1.0.2

- Installs to its own folder ("Updater for NoteTOO"), so updating NoteTOO can no longer interrupt it.
- Deletes the downloaded installer once the update is installed and checked.
