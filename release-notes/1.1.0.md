## Tables in notes

- Insert a table in the Scratchpad or a subject's notes with the new **Table** button: pick rows × columns.
- Type in cells, add or delete rows and columns, drag a column border to resize it, delete a table.
- Tables are saved as ordinary Markdown tables, so your notes stay readable everywhere.

## A smarter AI assistant

- Reads documents by their structure: chapters, sections, definitions, examples, lists and tables.
- Answers from the right passages and says where they are (page and section), keeps what the document says apart
  from its own interpretation, and tells you when the document does not say.
- Understands "this section", "the document I opened earlier", or a file you name, and remembers which document you
  are talking about across questions.
- **Highlight important** is far more selective and precise: it highlights the exact sentence, line by line, skips
  repetition and examples, never covers your own highlights, and you can still keep or discard each suggestion.
- Ask for what you need: "highlight the definitions", "surligne les dates clés".
